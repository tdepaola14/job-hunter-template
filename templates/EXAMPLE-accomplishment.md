# Adjustable Monitor Stand - Design and Manufacturing Project

## Overview

Two-person project for a sophomore design course, Spring 2025. Designed and
built a height-adjustable monitor stand that holds a 24 inch display and stores
a keyboard underneath. I owned the mechanism design and the CAD; my partner
owned the base structure and the finish work. Built one working prototype.

## Technologies

SOLIDWORKS, laser cutter, 3D printer (FDM, PLA), hand tools, calipers, birch
plywood, M4 hardware, gas spring.

## Skills

Mechanism design, tolerance analysis, engineering drawings, design for
manufacturability, iterative prototyping, hand fabrication, teamwork on a shared
CAD assembly.

## STAR Breakdown

- **Situation:** The course required a piece of furniture that adjusts to two
  distinct working heights and survives daily use.
- **Task:** Design the height mechanism so it holds position under load without
  a locking pin the user has to fiddle with.
- **Action:** Modeled the full assembly in SOLIDWORKS and sized a gas spring
  against the measured weight of the display and mount. Ran a tolerance stack on
  the sliding channel and opened the clearance from 0.2 to 0.5 mm after the
  first printed guide bound up. Cut the plywood panels on the laser cutter and
  printed the guides and end caps. Produced dimensioned drawings for every
  fabricated part so my partner could cut without asking me for numbers.
  Assembled the prototype and adjusted the spring preload over three iterations
  until it held both positions.
- **Result:** The finished stand holds either height without a pin and has been
  in daily use since. It carried the display plus about 3 kg of added weight in
  testing. We never instrumented the force required to adjust it, so how close
  the spring sizing came to the calculation is unknown.

## Resume Bullet Candidates

## Measurable Outcomes

Adjustment force was never measured, so the gas spring sizing remains a
calculation rather than a verified result. State it that way.

- Supports a 24 inch display plus 3 kg of added test weight.
- Slide clearance corrected from 0.2 mm (binding) to 0.5 mm (smooth).
- Three prototype revisions across a ten-week term.

## Supporting Details

- The gas spring was selected from a catalog against a calculated moment, not
  designed from scratch.
- The first laser-cut base cracked at a fastener boss; the second revision moved
  the boss 12 mm inboard and added a backing plate.
- My partner did the sanding and finishing, so the finish quality is not my work
  to claim in an interview.
